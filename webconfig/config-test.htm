Shortcuts
Two ways to change one. Click it and hold the combination you want, then let go - up to two keys besides the modifiers, and Esc on its own cancels. Or press Pick and assemble it without pressing anything. These are read by the board your keyboard is plugged into, and act on that board. Each one needs a key besides the modifiers, except Slow mouse, which the firmware is built without one for: modifiers on their own match everything typed while they are held, and would take the shortcuts below them with it. Config mode is listed but cannot be changed. It is the only way to reach this page, and this page is the only way to undo a shortcut, so it stays on what the firmware was built with. Two shortcuts cannot share a combination either - the second would never fire. Pick is the only way to reach the combinations the browser keeps for itself and never passes on to a page. In Chrome and Edge those are Ctrl T, N, W and their Shift forms, Ctrl Tab, Ctrl Shift Tab, Ctrl Page Up / Page Down, Ctrl 1-9, Ctrl L, F12 and Ctrl Shift I/J/C, plus anything the operating system takes first, such as Alt Tab or Alt F4. The device has no such reservations, so a shortcut set here works whatever the browser thinks of it.

Wipe Config erases the stored configuration and restores the compiled-in defaults. It cannot be undone from this page.

To upgrade the firmware, copy a .uf2 onto the DESKHOP drive this page came from. The device verifies it, flashes and reboots, then carries the new firmware over to the other board, blinking its LED throughout.
