Shortcuts
Two ways to change one. Click it and hold the combination you want, then let go - up to two keys besides the modifiers, and Esc on its own cancels. Or press Pick and assemble it without pressing anything. These are read by the board the keyboard is plugged into and take effect on that board - config mode, for one, brings its drive up on that board's computer rather than the one you happen to be looking at. Pick is the only way to reach the combinations the browser keeps for itself and never passes on to a page. In Chrome and Edge those are Ctrl T, N, W and their Shift forms, Ctrl Tab, Ctrl Shift Tab, Ctrl Page Up / Page Down, Ctrl 1-9, Ctrl L, F12 and Ctrl Shift I/J/C, plus anything the operating system takes first, such as Alt Tab or Alt F4. The device has no such reservations, so a shortcut set here works whatever the browser thinks of it.

Diagnostics
Destructive

Wipe Config erases the stored configuration and restores the compiled-in defaults. It cannot be undone from this page.

To upgrade the firmware, copy a .uf2 onto the DESKHOP drive this page came from. The device verifies it, flashes and reboots, then carries the new firmware over to the other board, blinking its LED throughout.
